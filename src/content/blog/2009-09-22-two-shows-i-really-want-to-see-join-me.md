I've always wanted to see a show at[ Cafe Eleven](http://www.cafeeleven.com/wordpress/) in St. Augustine. It just seems like a great venue with a cool group of people who support them.

I missed two shows last year that I'm really kicking myself for, Vampire Weekend and Against Me! Now is the time to redeem myself. I plan on seeing [Junior Boys](http://www.cafeeleven.com/wordpress/2009/04/junior-boys/) in October and [Elvis Perkins](http://www.cafeeleven.com/wordpress/2009/04/elvis-perkins/) in November.

I have found that October-November are the months to catch good shows in North Florida. Last year I got a chance to see [The Faint w/ The Show's the Rainbow](http://zoozical.com/2008/10/29/concert-revew-the-faint/) and then followed it up the next day with some [Portugal. The Man](http://zoozical.com/2008/11/04/).
