Back in 2016, I was camping with my friends Ben and Shawn. It was getting to be late one night, the fire was going.

### The last event - Resonant Frequencies

We had the goal of bringing together art and music in a jazzy (improv) kinda way. There was no pre-planning on what the music would be or how the art would turn out.  

Anthony Anurca was our amazing host who let us use his magical house for the event, as well as lead the music side of the event. Anthony is the basoonist for the Jacksonville Symphony and is known for his experimental synth tunes - as I said, magical.
