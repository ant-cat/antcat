but I couldn't stop dancing and in the middle of my jig I stopped to think about the lyrics

To me it just sounds like if she's hangin at your crib you might need to keep the plunger nearby....I'm just saying...i don't know how he made money on this!

Gross and inappropriate ... I know, but I'm not the one passing it off for music.
