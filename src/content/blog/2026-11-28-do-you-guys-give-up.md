Commitment is giving in. Sometimes you have to just give up and give in.... give in to the commitment. That feeling of not wanting to go, or do that thing, or not push yourself is a side of yourself fighting for its life.

Some people call this commitment but you can uncommit. I'm not strong enough to grip onto something that long. It's not sustainable for me. Instead I have to picture this as a falling into a deep well. But I have to choose to fall into a place that is designed to have beneficial outcomes. Working out, doing that project, being present with my little, being there for my partner/wife, not checking out. Fall into the well of giving in to the things that you know are the wise decisions.
