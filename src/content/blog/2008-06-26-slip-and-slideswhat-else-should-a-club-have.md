This **Saturday, June 28,** is the 3rd annual "[Wet Hot American Summer](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0243655/)" bash at the bar/club that I work at, [TSI](http://www.clubtsi.com). This is a party that we throw every year based on the movie,"[Wet Hot American Summer](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0243655/)". Good name for the party right. Well,  **super excited** is what I am. Friends and I just finished building a lifeguard chair to sit in and check ID's. The chair is stable and the brightest orange you can find. We are going to have [slip and slides](http://images.google.com/images?gbv=2&hl=en&q=slip+and+slide), [kiddie pools](http://images.google.com/images?gbv=2&hl=en&q=kiddie+pool&btnG=Search+Images), beer, champagne, short shorts, and a whole mess of 1981 memorabilia If you are out and about in Jacksonille on Saturday stop and we'll take care of you.
