I recently changed the name of my blog...I'm now on version 4.0....that's right, 4. Here they are in order:

	- Zoozical
	- Catknees
	- Anthony Has Ideas
	- OkNowWhat

Each name represents a different phase in my life, but I was never really happy with any of them until now. Why? What's in a name?

What "is" in a name? Who really gives a crap?

I do.

I was very proud of the "Anthony Has Ideas" moniker...[because it was given to me](http://oknowwh.at/2013/03/22/25-are-brilliant-ideas/).  But, it was given to me...something felt fundamentally wrong about the title "Anthony Has Ideas" and it wasn't my idea. It was more like "Anthony Rides On the Waves of Others."  I was really self-conscious about it. I felt like[ Jimmy from Seinfeld](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Apa0nG1OfUc), and  it went against one of my life learnings - if you have to say you're cool, you're probably not.

That brings us to the current name.

The other night I introduced my wife to the original "[Karate Kid](http://www.imdb.com/title/tt0087538/)" and the wisdom of Mr. Myagi ([Pat Morita](https://www.google.com/search?q=pat+morita&oq=pat+morita&aqs=chrome..69i57j69i60l5.2865j0&sourceid=chrome&espv=210&es_sm=91&ie=UTF-8)).  It's awesome to re-watch movies that you grew up with. You pick-up meanings and stories within the main plot that weren't caught originally. In the Karate Kid, there's a subtle but important point about the origin of Karate, Myagi, and Okinawa.

That's when I was able to use [my super powers](http://oknowwh.at/2013/10/14/dirty-deeds-thunder-cheeks/) and mishear Okinawa as "Ok Now What". Yes!...that's it!

It felt right, especially when the following lines came up during the [bonsai tree scene](http://youtu.be/GcdnMaQ68a8).
> **Mr. Myagi:**  "Trust the picture."
> **Daniel:** "How do I know if my picture is the right one?"
> **Mr. Myagi:** "If it comes from inside you, always right one."
Then I knew it was right.
