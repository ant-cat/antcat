New Bar/club in Jacksonville, FL: Dos Gatos! across the street from the [Florida theatre](http://maps.google.com/maps?f=q&source=s_q&hl=en&geocode=&q=128+E+Forsyth+St+%23+300+jacksonville,+fl&sll=30.342954,-81.587906&sspn=0.161776,0.261955&ie=UTF8&hq=&hnear=128+E+Forsyth+St,+Jacksonville,+Duval,+Florida+32202&ll=30.327268,-81.65554&spn=0.010576,0.016372&z=16&iwloc=A&layer=c&cbll=30.326516,-81.655502&panoid=sGWImKwz0aTCJkN2A143dQ&cbp=12,208.27,,0,5)

It was great! I didn't have one thing to say that I didn't like. The owner J. has really done a great job of picking out an awesome staff with great decor.

I may have found my new favorite place! So go and check it out!!!
